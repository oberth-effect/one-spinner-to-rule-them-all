# Sources

Every line in the plugin's default list comes from a passage in J.R.R. Tolkien's legendarium.
This page gives each line's original wording, who says it, and where it is.

## How the lines were made

- Each line is written in the -ing form that Claude Code's own spinner verbs use.
- **Original wording** is copied exactly from the edition listed below, and was checked word for word against it.
- **as written** means the line appears in the book unchanged.
- **reworded** means only the verb form, word order or a pronoun changed, or words were left out.
- **chapter title** means the line turns a chapter title into the -ing form.
- Nothing comes from the films. Lines found only in the films were left out.

## Editions

- *The Lord of the Rings*, 50th anniversary edition
- *The Silmarillion*, edited by Christopher Tolkien, illustrated by Ted Nasmith
- *Unfinished Tales*, edited by Christopher Tolkien
- *The Children of Húrin*, edited by Christopher Tolkien
- *Beren and Lúthien*, edited by Christopher Tolkien
- *The Fall of Gondolin*, edited by Christopher Tolkien

The quotations are short excerpts, given for attribution. The works remain under copyright of the Tolkien Estate.
